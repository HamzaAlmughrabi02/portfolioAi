# Product

## Register
brand

## Users
Recruiters and engineering hiring teams assessing Hamza Almughrabi for machine learning, data science and analytics roles. The user confirmed employment as the primary goal.

## Product Purpose
Present verified project work, relevant skills, education and training, with direct access to source code, the CV and professional contact details.

## Brand Personality
Precise, intelligent, confident. Preserve the existing command-center composition and the supplied black and lime circuit identity.

## Anti-references
Avoid invented seniority, decorative performance claims, illegible terminal text, distracting animation and unrelated magenta accents. Preserve the established layout rather than applying a new template.

## Design Principles
Lead with the professional role. Show evidence through projects. Keep navigation direct. Make reading and contacting effortless. Keep every claim grounded in the CV.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Readable mobile typography, visible keyboard focus, meaningful link labels, reduced motion support and sufficient text contrast.
